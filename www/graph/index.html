Graph explorer

SummaryThe knowledge graph of this node and, when you ask, of the peer nodes it names, drawn in your browser from their published files: search it, list it, read each node's summary and relations, and explore any other node by its address.

Every node of the Agentic Knowledge Web publishes its knowledge as a graph, graph.jsonld, named with its digest in the node's discovery document. This page reads that file and draws it. Tick Peers to read the graph of each peer node the document names and draw them together. Give it another node's address to explore that node instead.

      How it works

      The page reads the node's discovery document, takes the address of its graph and the digest declared for it, reads the graph and checks the digest of the bytes it received. When Peers is ticked, it then follows each …/rel#peer link of the document and does the same for that node, and draws a link between the two nodes, and wherever an item of one names an item of the other. It follows at most 16 peers of each node, reads at most 10 MB of each file and draws at most 5,000 nodes and 10,000 links, and says in the status line what it left out.

      The page only reads published files: the discovery documents, the graphs and, for a selected item, the chunk export. It sends nothing else and keeps nothing except the width you give the side panel, in your browser's local storage.

      With a keyboard: Tab reaches each node in the side panel, Enter selects it and centres the picture on it, and Escape clears the selection or closes a summary. The bar between the picture and the panel resizes the panel: drag it, tap or click it to step through three widths, or use the arrow keys, Home and End.
