Brief Desk

SummarySearch the passages a knowledge node publishes, check each against its published digest, and collect cited cards in a brief. Do it by hand, or ask the assistant: two published agents that run in your browser on your own AI provider key, and that ask before they discard anything or contact another site.

Ask a question, or search by hand. Every passage this page shows comes from a file a knowledge node publishes, and each one is checked against the digest that node published for it before it goes into your brief. The assistant below runs in your browser, on the AI provider and key you choose; this site has no server behind it and never receives your key.

      Ask the Brief Desk

      The assistant searches the nodes you allowed, reads passages, checks each against its published digest, answers with the passage's address, and adds or removes cards when you ask.

      Your messages and the passages the assistant reads go to the AI provider you choose in the settings below. You confirm anything that cannot be undone before it happens, and you are asked before another site's assistant is contacted.

      Connecting the assistant… If this message stays, the assistant could not load. You can still use the page directly.

      How this page works

      • The agents are published data. The network of two agents is the file brief_desk.network.json, named by agent-manifest.json; its instructions are public. Another agentic website can load it by the address https://agenticsystemcore.com/desk/brief_desk and run it in its own visitor's browser.
      • The agents run in your browser. The runtime in /desk/runtime/ is the open-source browser runtime of the Open Agentic Web project, unchanged. Model calls go from your browser to the provider you choose, with your key, which stays in this browser's storage for this site.
      • What the agents can do here is this page's own list. Six actions: read the page's state, search passages, read one passage and check its digest, add a card, remove a card, discard the brief. Each change to your brief shows you what it changes and asks first. Another site that runs this network gets two of them, search and read with the digest check, over this node's published passages only.
      • Every card is checked. A node publishes each passage with the SHA-256 digest of its text in /chunks.jsonl. The page computes the digest of the text it received and adds the card only when the two are equal. This shows that the text is the published text, not that it is true.
      • Other sites are asked only with your consent. The page reads another node's passages after you tick it. The assistant can also ask the assistant of patterns.agenticsystemcore.com or standards.agenticsystemcore.com; a card names the site and shows the request before anything is sent.

      The page works without the assistant, and without a key: the search form and the brief use the same code the assistant calls. The source is in the site's repository, under Apache-2.0.
