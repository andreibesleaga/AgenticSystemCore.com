What the extension reads from a site and why

SummaryThe public files WebAIAssist reads from a site you allowed, why it reads each, what stays in the browser and what can leave.

In short

  • WebAIAssist reads a short, fixed list of things a site publishes about itself, to show you what the site offers and to give the model a few facts.
  • It reads them for a site you allowed, or after you click the toolbar button on that page. Nothing is read in the background or from other tabs.
  • Requests go to the open site as a plain GET with no cookies and no referrer, and no redirect is followed.
  • With a model in the browser or Chrome's built-in model, your questions, page text and tool results stay on your computer. A remote chat endpoint receives them.
  • It does not read your cookies, other tabs, your history or files on your computer.

WebAIAssist reads a short, fixed list of things that a site publishes about itself. It reads them to show you what the site offers and to give the model a few facts, so the model asks the page's tools instead of guessing. Each thing it reads is in the standards table, with its status.

When it reads

  • Only for a site you allowed. Pressing Allow in the panel asks Chrome for access to that one site. At install the extension has access to no site.
  • Or after you click the toolbar button on a page. Chrome then lets the extension work on that tab until it closes or moves to another site.
  • Nothing is read in the background, for pages you did not open, or for other tabs.

What it reads

All requests go to the open site only, as a plain GET with no cookies and no referrer. Each reply is size-limited and must have the expected type.

"Nothing" in the last column means the file is shown to you only. What the model does get is given in short form, unless you turn that off in Options ("Give the model short facts from what the site publishes"). Everything from a site is marked as untrusted data: no file can add a tool, change a setting or name an address to fetch.

The exact paths, size limits and code are in STANDARDS.md and src/core/site-files.ts.

What stays in the browser

  • Your questions, the page text you read, the tool results and the model's answers stay on your computer when the model runs in the browser (graphics card or processor) or is Chrome's built-in model.
  • SPARQL queries over a node's graph run in your browser. The query text and the rows are not sent anywhere by the extension.
  • Saved chats and tool counts live in the extension's own storage. Forget this site and Forget all sites remove them. There is no account and no analytics.

You tick another node in the graph explorerUp to three published files are read from that node (an https site with a public name).
A site says ai-input=noNothing of the site's page text or files goes to the model. Its tool list and the results of its tools you allow still go to the model you chose, including a remote endpoint.

Results of a site's own tools still go to the model you chose, because you asked for the call. The full list of hosts and every stored item is in PRIVACY.md.

What it does not read

It does not read your cookies, other tabs, your history, files on your computer or any site you did not allow. It does not read the paths it does not list. It follows no redirect for a site file.
